**Personal responsibility.** I voluntarily choose to use Forge. Forge does not require me to perform any exercise, eat any food, follow any nutrition target, or act on any recommendation. I am responsible for deciding whether and how to follow its guidance, considering my health, abilities, circumstances, and professional advice. I may decline a recommendation or stop participating at any time.

**No medical or professional nutrition care.** Forge and its employees do not act as my physicians, registered dietitians, licensed nutrition professionals, or other healthcare providers. Using Forge does not establish a healthcare-provider relationship. Forge provides general educational and wellness information informed by publicly available research, published guidance, and general fitness and nutrition principles. Its content, including AI-generated recommendations and food estimates, may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or unsuitable for me. Published research does not guarantee that a recommendation is safe or effective for my circumstances.

**STOP IMMEDIATELY IF YOU FEEL DISCOMFORT.** If at any time while using Forge or following its exercise, nutrition, or other guidance I experience discomfort, pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, or any other concerning symptom, I will stop the activity or recommendation immediately and seek medical advice from my doctor before resuming. I will not push through symptoms or rely on Forge to determine whether it is safe to continue. If symptoms are severe or suggest an emergency, I will seek emergency medical care immediately rather than wait for a routine appointment.

**Assumption of risk.** I understand that exercise and changes in eating may cause injury, illness, or other harm, including serious injury or death. I voluntarily assume the risks associated with activities and dietary choices I undertake while using Forge. I am responsible for using equipment safely, reviewing food and portion estimates, obtaining appropriate professional guidance, and stopping when symptoms or circumstances make continued participation unsafe. I will not use Forge to disregard medical advice or delay necessary care.

**Release of claims.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, I release **[full legal name of Forge's operating company]**, its officers, employees, and contractors from claims for injury, illness, death, or other loss arising from my voluntary participation in exercise or dietary activities undertaken in reliance on Forge's general fitness and nutrition content, **including claims arising from their ordinary negligence in providing that content**. For claims validly covered by this release, I waive my right to recover damages or pursue legal action against those released parties.

This release does not cover gross negligence, reckless or intentional misconduct, fraud, privacy or data-security claims, or any liability or rights that applicable law does not permit me to waive.

**Acceptance before personal use.** I have read and understand this agreement, including its assumption of risk and release of claims. I understand that I am giving up certain legal rights to the extent permitted by law. I voluntarily accept this agreement before accessing Forge's personal fitness and nutrition features.

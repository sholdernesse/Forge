Forge is not a doctor, registered dietitian, physical therapist, or emergency service. It does not diagnose or treat a medical condition or replace individualized professional care.

**Your health and choices.** Physical activity and changes in eating can involve injury or other health risks, including strains, falls, overexertion, and adverse effects from an unsuitable training or nutrition choice. I am responsible for deciding whether an activity is appropriate for me and for using equipment safely. I may stop an activity at any time. I will not use Forge's output as a reason to ignore pain, injury, illness, or medical advice. If I have a condition, injury, pregnancy-related concern, eating-disorder history, or take medication that may affect exercise, appetite, hydration, or nutrition, I will consult an appropriate qualified professional before following a recommendation. If I experience severe symptoms or an emergency, I will stop and seek urgent care.

**Understanding the limits.** I understand that Forge may change a plan based on my check-ins and logs, but it cannot observe everything about my health or technique. Exercise illustrations are instructional aids, not a live assessment of my form. Food-photo and barcode results require my review and may misidentify foods or portions; I will check them before saving a log or using them for a health decision. Optional medication-related personalization is general wellness support, not dosing or treatment advice.

**Acknowledgment and voluntary participation.** I have read this agreement, understand the risks and limits above, and choose to use Forge's fitness and nutrition features voluntarily.

# Sprint 4.77 — Prone Y Raise Visual Guide

## Goal

Add paired movement and anatomical guidance for the highest-use uncovered upper-body movement in the active planner.

## Delivered

- Added an original two-position AI-character image for the chest-supported prone Y raise.
- Added a separate rear three-quarter sports-anatomy illustration highlighting the lower trapezius and rear deltoids, with supporting middle trapezius, rhomboids, rotator cuff, and trunk stabilizers.
- Added setup, movement, common-mistake, self-check, tempo, breathing, regression, and safety coaching.
- Distinguished the diagonal Y path from a row, shrug, or lateral T raise.
- Added focused catalog and movement-contract tests.

## Product constraint

The guide uses the existing `prone-y-raise` identifier already prescribed in both dumbbell and bodyweight upper-body plans. It adds no screen, setting, route, or data field.

The movement and muscle assets remain separate, and the shared green/amber muscle colors remain provisional pending the later system-wide visual review.

## Acceptance

- The chest remains visibly supported in both movement positions.
- The raised arms form a clear symmetrical Y with thumbs up and shoulders away from the ears.
- The anatomical view exposes the rear shoulder and scapular regions from a useful coaching angle.
- Guidance begins unloaded and avoids prescribing a forced height.

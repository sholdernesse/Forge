# Sprint 4.76 — Reverse Lunge Visual Guide

## Goal

Give band and bodyweight lower-body plans a dedicated reverse-lunge guide instead of implying that the stationary split-squat visual teaches the same movement.

## Delivered

- Added an original two-position AI-character image showing the feet-together start and controlled step-back position.
- Added a separate rear three-quarter sports-anatomy illustration for the lead-leg quadriceps and glutes, with supporting hamstrings, adductors, calves, and core.
- Added setup, movement, mistake, self-check, tempo, breathing, regression, and safety guidance.
- Made step direction, foot tracks, lead-foot pressure, knee tracking, and the return-to-balance transition observable.
- Added focused automated catalog and movement-contract coverage.

## Product constraint

The guide uses the existing `reverse-lunge` planner identifier, so it appears in both band and bodyweight lower-body plans without adding another screen, setting, or exercise variant.

The movement and anatomy assets remain separate, and muscle colors remain provisional pending the later cross-guide visual review.

## Acceptance

- The movement visual clearly begins from a tall stance and steps backward.
- The guide does not depict a walking-forward or rear-foot-elevated lunge.
- The anatomical companion uses a rear three-quarter view to expose lead-leg and supporting muscle regions.
- Coaching distinguishes the balance transition from a stationary split squat and offers stable support as a regression.

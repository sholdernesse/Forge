# Sprint 4.73 — Push-up Visual Guide

## Outcome

Bodyweight training plans now include complete push-up guidance with separate range-of-motion and anatomical illustrations.

## Delivered

- Adds a two-panel movement image showing a high-plank start and controlled bottom position.
- Adds a separate front three-quarter anatomical view emphasizing chest and triceps.
- Identifies front deltoids, serratus anterior, and core as supporting muscles.
- Teaches a single head-to-heel body line, approximately 30–45-degree elbow path, controlled depth, and slow descent.
- Provides an elevated stable-surface option when floor repetitions cannot remain controlled.
- Connects both assets to the existing `push-up` planner identifier.
- Adds regression coverage for both assets, primary muscles, body-line cues, elbow path, and regression option.

## Boundaries

- Depth follows repeatable body and shoulder control rather than a forced chest-to-floor rule.
- The guide does not diagnose wrist, elbow, shoulder, chest, or back symptoms.
- Generated imagery remains subordinate to the written movement and safety guidance.

## Acceptance

- Both positions preserve a straight head-to-heel line.
- The chest lowers between the hands without excessive elbow flare.
- The anatomy view prioritizes the chest and triceps over supporting muscles.
- Type checks, full tests, and the production build pass.

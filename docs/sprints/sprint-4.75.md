# Sprint 4.75 — Dumbbell Split Squat Visual Guide

## Goal

Extend Forge's paired movement-and-anatomy guidance to the highest-use uncovered strength movement in the active planner.

## Delivered

- Added an original two-position AI-character dumbbell split squat image showing a stationary staggered stance and controlled depth.
- Added a separate rear three-quarter anatomical illustration highlighting the lead-leg quadriceps and glutes, with supporting hamstrings, adductors, calves, and core.
- Added setup, movement, mistake, self-check, tempo, breathing, and safety coaching.
- Made stance width, knee tracking, lead-foot pressure, and repeatable stationary positioning observable without prescribing a universal depth.
- Added automated catalog and movement-specific acceptance coverage.

## Product constraint

The guide uses the existing `split-squat` planner identifier, so the visual appears in both barbell-equipped and dumbbell-only lower-body plans without adding a new route, control, or data field.

Muscle colors remain provisional pending the planned cross-guide visual-system review.

## Acceptance

- The movement and anatomy visuals remain separate.
- Both visuals show the same stationary dumbbell split-squat movement rather than a walking lunge or rear-foot-elevated variation.
- The anatomical view exposes the posterior and medial support muscles while retaining the shared Forge primary/supporting color language.
- The guide remains usable with bodyweight or lighter load when balance or control requires regression.

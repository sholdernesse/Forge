# Sprint 4.88 — Honest Hydration Context

## Goal

Give the existing water log useful evidence-based context without presenting a general total-water reference as a personalized plain-water prescription.

## Delivered

- Added the established adult total-water reference beside today's plain-water log.
- Use the onboarding profile when it supports the standard male or female reference; otherwise show the full adult reference range.
- Explain that food and all beverages count toward total water.
- Keep the existing 8 fl oz and 16 fl oz quick actions, undo behavior, persistence, and layout.
- Added focused coverage for male, female, and unspecified profile contexts.

## Evidence and safety

- The reference follows the U.S. National Academies adequate intake used by the [CDC/NCHS water-intake report](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db242.htm): 3.7 L for adult men and 2.7 L for adult women from all foods and liquids.
- The [CDC water guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/water-healthy-drinks/index.html) notes that needs vary with factors including activity, climate, fever, vomiting, and diarrhea.
- Forge does not compare its plain-water log directly with the total-water reference, label the user dehydrated, or recommend rapid fluid intake.
- Medical conditions, pregnancy, medications, and clinician-directed fluid limits remain outside this general product guidance.

## Product constraint

This context stays inside the existing hydration quick log. It adds no card, route, questionnaire, service, or stored field.

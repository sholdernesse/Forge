# Sprint 4.78 — Bodyweight Squat Visual Guide

## Goal

Give new and equipment-free users a complete visual foundation for the squat pattern before Forge introduces loaded variations.

## Delivered

- Added an original two-position AI-character bodyweight-squat image.
- Added a separate rear three-quarter sports-anatomy illustration highlighting quadriceps and glutes, with supporting hamstrings, adductors, calves, back, and core.
- Added setup, movement, common-mistake, self-check, tempo, breathing, regression, and safety coaching.
- Made tripod-foot pressure, knee tracking, balance, and self-selected pain-free depth observable.
- Added focused catalog and movement-contract tests.

## Product constraint

The guide uses the existing `bodyweight-squat` planner identifier and appears automatically for equipment-free lower-body plans. It adds no screen, route, setting, or stored state.

The movement and anatomy images remain distinct. Green and amber muscle colors remain provisional pending the later visual-system review.

## Acceptance

- Both feet remain visible and planted in each movement position.
- The squat demonstrates a controlled, approachable depth rather than an extreme endpoint.
- Coaching does not claim that one stance or depth is correct for every user.
- A stable support and shallower range are offered as regressions.

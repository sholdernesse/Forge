# Sprint 4.65 — Planned Consolidation Week

## Outcome

Approved later training blocks now include a predictable Week 4 consolidation so Forge manages accumulated fatigue before asking users to progress again.

## Delivered

- Keeps the first foundation block unchanged.
- Labels Week 4 of Block 2 and later as `Consolidate`.
- Reduces planned working sets by 25% and load by 10% during that week.
- Retains the deeper 35% set reduction when recovery or recent feedback indicates fatigue.
- Keeps stopped-for-discomfort feedback authoritative by selecting recovery instead of loaded training.
- Explains the active consolidation directly beside today's workout.
- Adds block-timing and deload-depth regression coverage.

## Boundaries

- Consolidation lasts only from days 21 through 27 of an approved later block.
- Forge does not add a separate screen, questionnaire, or user action.
- Active workout locking continues to prevent a generated plan from replacing an in-progress session.
- Users still explicitly approve every later block.

## Acceptance

- Foundation Week 4 remains a review phase.
- Later-block Week 4 reduces work predictably and explains why.
- Evidence-driven fatigue can make the reduction more conservative.
- Type checks, full tests, and production builds pass.

# Sprint 4.91 — One-Tap Recent Meals

## Goal

Reduce repeated food-entry work without adding a prescriptive meal-planning workflow.

## Delivered

- Detects distinct previously logged breakfasts, lunches, dinners, and snacks.
- Shows up to three recent meals for the currently selected meal type in most-recent order.
- Repeats every food, serving, macro, and verified nutrient field into today’s log with one action.
- Preserves manually saved meals for intentional favorites while making ordinary repeat meals available without setup.
- Added focused coverage for selection, ordering, meal filtering, totals, and safe entry duplication.

## Product constraint

Forge reuses what the user actually logged. It does not invent a meal plan, claim dietary suitability, or fill missing days automatically.
